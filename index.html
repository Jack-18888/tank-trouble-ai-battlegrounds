Mind the ricochet. Each tank can have five shells in flight. Shells bounce off walls, last about eight seconds, and can destroy the tank that fired them after a bounce.

Pick your trouble. Easy bots react slowly. Medium bots navigate the maze. Hard bots dodge shells and calculate bank shots. Choose a controller for every tank: one human at most, or all bots to watch them battle.
